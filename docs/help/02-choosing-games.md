# Choosing your games

## The simple and full Library

The Library starts simple: a search box, your games and the copy bar.
**More options** opens the full page, with its filters, featured groups,
layouts and themes. **Fewer options** goes back, and the app remembers
which you chose. If filters from the full page are still on, the simple
page says so and offers **Clear filters**.

## What gets dropped

Before a game reaches the library, the filters remove what isn't a
playable arcade game: BIOS sets, devices, mechanical machines, and
games you've chosen to leave out, such as mature content or games
whose text is Japanese-only. Change these under **Settings → Filters**.

**Hide compilations** leaves out boards that hold several games at once.
It is off until you turn it on, in the guide or under **Settings → Filters**.

If the library says "No games match your filters", try a different
search or clear the filters. **More options** shows every filter.

## One version per game

Many games exist in several versions: regions, revisions and bootlegs.
The app picks one automatically. It prefers a version the community
rates well, then the parent over a clone, then better emulation, then
your preferred region, then the latest revision.

## Choosing a different version

Click a game to open its drawer. It lists every version, marks the one
the app picked, and lets you choose another with one click. A game you
have changed shows a **Manually overridden** badge. A game with other
versions shows **Has alternative versions**.

## Choosing a game's picture

A game sometimes shows the wrong picture, or none. Open its drawer and
choose **Fix this game's picture or version, step by step**, then
**A different picture**. A game with one version goes straight to its
pictures.

The guide shows every picture it finds, large, with what it shows and
where it came from. Choose one, and the Library shows it at once. Your
choice is for that version only. **Look in more places** also asks the
online sources that limit how often they may be asked. **Use the usual
picture** undoes your choice. Choices are saved in a Backup.

## Sessions

A session saves what the Library is showing: its year range, and the
genre, publisher or developer chosen, under a name like `80s shooters`.
A search and the other filters are not saved.
Switch between sessions on the **Sessions** page without losing your
place.
